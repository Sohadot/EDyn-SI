The central thesis / EDyn.si
The most consequential question may not be how intelligent a system is today, but how its intelligence can change.

EDyn connects evolutionary dynamics with the study of artificial superintelligence. It offers a concise conceptual home for questions of capability transition, iterative improvement, evaluation, and the design of systems that could exceed present-day intelligence boundaries.
